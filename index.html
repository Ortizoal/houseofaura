Everything you need to know before choosing your next House of Aura piece.

How do I place an order?

Choose a piece from the shop, then send us an enquiry through the order form or WhatsApp. We’ll confirm availability, sizing and payment details personally.

What sizes are available?

Our pieces are available in selected sizes, with some styles made to order. Share your usual size and measurements in your enquiry so we can guide you.

How long does delivery take?

Orders within Accra usually arrive within 1–3 working days. Nationwide delivery typically takes 2–5 working days after your order is confirmed.

Can I request a custom piece?

Yes. For selected styles, we can discuss colour, fit and other small adjustments. Send us your idea and we’ll let you know what is possible.
